## N. Lahén — formation of dense star clusters in low-metallicity starbursts

Same **GRIFFIN** lineage as [[Partmann — IMBHs in dwarf galaxies]] — solar-mass resolution, individual massive stars, non-equilibrium chemistry.

Feeds the clustered-feedback story from [[Reina-Campos — SCALES]]: how the dense clusters form in the first place, where SCALES asks what they do once formed.

Relevant background: young massive clusters are globular-cluster-mass objects forming *today* in starbursts and mergers → [[Star clusters and NSCs]].

*Furthest from my own work of the afternoon block.*

## E. Shin — accretion disc to CGM: multiscale simulations of Blandford–Znajek jet feedback in dwarf galaxies

Connects to the spin thread: BZ jet power scales roughly as $a^2$ and requires the thick-disc / MAD configuration → [[Accretion disc states]], [[Equilibrium spin]].

Interesting angle: in dwarfs the potential is shallow, so BH feedback could couple unusually strongly. Complementary to [[Partmann — IMBHs in dwarf galaxies]], which asks about growth rather than feedback.

Question worth having: *at what BH mass does jet feedback start to matter in a dwarf, and does it self-limit the growth Partmann finds?*

## S. Buttigieg — the gravitational wave background: cosmological simulations vs pulsar timing observations

**Closes the loop.** Tests simulated BH merger populations against the PTA signal — the observational endpoint of everything in [[Trinca — multi-regime accretion disc]], [[Huško — hybrid AGN in COLIBRE]] and [[Johansson — KETJU]].

Key dependency: the PTA amplitude depends on merger rates, mass ratios, **and eccentricity at entry into the GW-driven regime** — the $(1-e^2)^{-7/2}$ scaling in [[Post-Newtonian expansion]]. KETJU gives eccentricities from integration; subgrid models assume them.

Question worth having: *how much of the predicted amplitude uncertainty comes from BH mass function vs merger timescales vs eccentricity?*

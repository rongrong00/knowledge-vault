# Literature

One note per source. Title the note after the source, not after your reaction to it.

**Naming:** `Author Year - Short Title` works well — e.g. `Furlanetto 2006 - Cosmology at Low Frequencies`.
It sorts chronologically per author and matches how you'll half-remember it.

Insert the **Literature Note** template (`Cmd+P` → Insert template).

Put the DOI and bibkey in the frontmatter — if you ever wire this up to Zotero,
that's the join key and you'll be glad it's already there.

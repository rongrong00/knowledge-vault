# Log

Dated notes, chronological, never reorganized. `Cmd+P` → "Open today's daily note".

Worth writing down: what you ran, what broke, what surprised you, what you decided
and why. Link out to `Concepts/` freely — the log becomes an index of your own
thinking, and the backlinks panel on any concept note shows you every day you
wrestled with it.

Low bar for entry. Three bullets is a fine day.

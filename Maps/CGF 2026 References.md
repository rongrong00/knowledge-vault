## People notes

- **Jeremiah (Jerry) Ostriker** (1937–2025) — Princeton; advisor was Chandrasekhar; students included Scott Tremaine, Ellen Zweibel, Ena Choi. Died April 2025, so colleagues will speak of him in the past tense.
- **Eve Ostriker** — his daughter; Lyman Spitzer Jr. Professor at Princeton. ISM and star formation, TIGRESS. Upstream of the resolved-SN and multiphase-ISM approaches in several talks here.
- **Ena Choi** — PhD Princeton 2013 under Jerry Ostriker; now University of Seoul. Mistral AGN feedback model in AREPO.
- **Flatiron Institute / CCA** — Simons Foundation research division, NYC. Director Julianne Dalcanton since 2021 (founded by Spergel 2016). CAMELS lives there. **CCA Pre-Doctoral Program** and **Flatiron Research Fellowships** (open ~mid-Sept, deadline ~end Oct) are worth tracking.
